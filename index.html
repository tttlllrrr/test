About

Taylor Shechet is a multidisciplinary creative technologist working across game audio, music, technical art, UX, real-time graphics, VR/AR, and emerging technology. His work combines artistic sensibility with technical problem-solving, with particular experience in Unreal Engine, real-time rendering, immersive display systems, and creative pipelines.

Taylor began working with VR in 2014 and AR in 2018, collaborating with artists, engineers, and companies including Niantic, Wave, Strangeloop Studios, and Sphere. He spent four years at Madison Square Garden's Sphere as a Senior Technical Artist for Real-Time Software, contributing to the launch of the Sphere, nDisplay pipelines, Unreal previsualization tools, visualization and content pipelines, technical research, and projects for the Exosphere.

His career spans UX, audio, game development, technical art, real-time graphics, spatial computing, and emerging technology. He is comfortable working across disciplines and building tools, pipelines, and creative solutions for ambiguous or technically unusual problems.

His current primary focus is indie game audio, while remaining open to technical-art and creative-technology opportunities. He is particularly interested in unusual intersections of audio, technical art, real-time technology, spatial computing, and creative problem-solving, as well as emerging fields including microrobotics and mycotechnology.
